# Phase 6 — Community Intelligence + Content Submission

Phase 6 turns the Community route into a moderated student-contribution system. Students can discover approved campus information and submit practical knowledge. Public publishing is never immediate: new or edited posts return to `pending` until an administrator approves them.

## Categories
- Campus tips
- Academics
- Transport
- Food
- Services
- Lost & found
- General

Events, opportunities, accommodation and safety remain separate domain modules rather than being duplicated inside Community.

## Trust workflow
Student submission -> validation -> pending -> administrator review -> approved/rejected -> public if approved.

Approved posts can be reported by students. Reports are reviewed by administrators and marked resolved or dismissed. Students can view their own submissions and moderation status, edit them (which sends them back to pending), or delete them.

## Privacy and role boundaries
Student routes require the student role. Administrator moderation routes require the administrator role. Community moderation does not provide access to private timetable, reminder or budget data.
